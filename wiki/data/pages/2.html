Prime Evils

Monsters with the Prime Evil tag all come with additional buffs. Prime Evil monsters include:

  • All act bosses, classic bosses and classic ubers
  • All map bosses
  • All dungeon monsters and bosses
  • All new ubers and their minions


The additional buffs that they gain are:

  • 200% damage to minions and mercenaries
  • Immune to slow effects (cold damage, Decrepify, Holy Freeze, Slows Target by X%, etc.)
  • Immune to Dim Vision, Terror, Confuse, and Attract
  • Immune to Sanctuary's physical resistance removal

Ubers

All ubers regenerate life like most monsters, but their high life means they regenerate substantially more per second than most. Monsters do not regenerate life while being damaged, so continuous damage such as Poison Damage or Open Wounds will halt their regeneration entirely. Prevent Monster Heal does not affect ubers. Note that ubers' immunities are not shown beneath their name like they are for other monsters.

Ubers within Uber Tristram no longer regenerate health

Uber Tristram Prime Evils

When the final prime evil is defeated, they drop a Hellfire Torch and a Black Soulstone.
  • Uber Tristram is now accessed with Pandemonium Talisman
  • All pre-buffs are removed when characters enter Uber Tristram
  • Players can no longer freely re-enter Uber Tristram while enemies remain within - when first entering the area, three single-use silver portals are created in town and the area may only be re-entered by using them (players get their original attempt plus three extra attempts)
  • Uber Mephisto uses level 20 Conviction (-50% resistances, was -125%)
  • Uber Baal now casts his own version of Lower Resist (-75% resistances, lasts about 25 seconds)
  • Uber Mephisto, Diablo, and Baal now have 10% more health and 25% less defense
  • These ubers no longer drop Standard of Heroes
Uber Diablo is sometimes referred to as "Pandemonium Diablo" with "Uber Diablo" being reserved for Diablo-Clone, but that nomenclature is avoided here.

Uber Ancients

  • Players can choose to fight 1, 2, or all 3 of the ancients by toggling their statues prior to starting the fight
  • When defeated, a Standard of Heroes will drop. If more than one ancient was fought, each additional ancient beyond the first adds a chance for another to drop.

Diablo-Clone

Diablo-Clone is an endgame boss accessed with a Vision of Terror. Defeating him awards an Annihilus.

Encounter overview

Entry Use a Vision of Terror at the Blood Fountain in Harrogath.
Difficulty Tier 0, Tier 1 or Tier 2 (change the tier by cubing the Vision of Terror with itself)
Main hazards Cold novas, Meteors, Bone Spirits which create dangerous patches of burning ground on collision, burning ground and skeleton waves
Entry effects Pre-buffs are removed and your mercenary is killed upon entering. Prepare to recast your buffs inside.
Re-entry Tier 0 provides three portals in town. If you die, you will respawn in town as normal and you can enter the arena again, consuming one of the portals. Entering a Tier 0 portal grants temporary invulnerability while you get your body. Re-entry is not possible in Tier 1 and 2
Exit You cannot exit the arena except by killing Diablo-Clone, saving and exiting, or dying.
Guaranteed reward Annihilus

Damage range: Diablo-Clone becomes immune to damage when no player is within approximately 10 yards of him. When in this state the Salvation aura graphic appears at his feet. Ranged characters must remain close enough to damage him.

Fight progression

The fight consists of fighting Diablo-Clone alone, three skeleton waves and three cold-nova sequences, triggered separately by Diablo-Clone's remaining life.

Remaining life Event What happens
Above 85% Opening combat Diablo-Clone uses his regular skills and attacks. Avoid meteors, Bone Spirits and burning ground while damaging him to trigger the first skeleton wave.
Below 85% Skeleton wave 1 Melee skeletons (Bone Warriors) are summoned. Diablo-Clone is immune while the wave remains alive. Defeat the skeletons, then resume damaging him.
Below 75% Cold-nova sequence 1 Diablo-Clone becomes immune, moves toward the centre and releases six cold novas. Meteors and Bone Spirits are not released during the cold-nova sequence.
After sequence 1, until below 60% Combat resumes Diablo-Clone becomes vulnerable and resumes his normal combat attacks. Meteors and Bone Spirits resume; avoid their projectiles and burning ground.
Below 60% Skeleton wave 2 Melee skeletons and Skeleton Archers are summoned. Diablo-Clone is immune while the wave remains alive. Defeat the skeletons, then resume damaging him.
Below 50% Cold-nova sequence 2 Another sequence of six cold novas. Diablo-Clone is immune during the sequence.
After sequence 2, until below 35% Combat resumes Diablo-Clone becomes vulnerable and resumes his normal combat attacks, with meteors, Bone Spirits and burning ground remaining hazards.
Below 35% Skeleton wave 3 Melee skeletons, Skeleton Archers and skeleton mages are summoned. Diablo-Clone is immune while the wave remains alive. Defeat the skeletons, then resume damaging him.
Below 25% Cold-nova sequence 3 The final sequence of six cold novas. Diablo-Clone is immune during the sequence.
After sequence 3 Final combat Diablo-Clone becomes vulnerable and resumes his normal combat attacks. Meteors, Bone Spirits and burning ground remain dangerous. No further scheduled skeleton waves or cold-nova sequences remain.

The transition cold novas are separate from his normal fire nova. Despite their internal skill name, UberDiabSuperFire, the transition novas deal cold damage.

Cold novas and pillar positioning

The transition novas deal cold damage.

  • Diablo-Clone will gain the Salvation aura visual and accompanying sound, and move toward the centre before casting. This is your cue to seek cover. If blocked, he will teleport to the center.
  • Each sequence contains six novas. Successive novas become more dangerous.
  • The novas consist of outward-moving projectiles that pillars (or bodies) can intercept.
  • Cold resistance helps against cold damage, but hiding behind a pillar is the intended way to avoid these projectiles.

Rewards

Defeating Diablo-Clone guarantees an Annihilus. Tier 1 and Tier 2 also have a 1 in 200 chance to drop one of five exclusive unique items. The exclusive items are Overlord's Helm, Dark Abyss, Itherael's Path, Hadriel's Hand and Aidan's Scar.

Rathma and Mendeln

Rathma and Mendeln are endgame bosses accessed with a Voidstone. The encounter includes three clearing stages and three boss fights. Defeating the final pair awards a Tainted Worldstone Shard. "Rathma" can refer to the actual boss, but is a colloquial term for this fight as a whole.

Encounter overview

Entry Use a Voidstone at the Blood Fountain in Harrogath.
Difficulty Tier 0, Tier 1 or Tier 2 (change the tier by cubing the Voidstone with itself).
Main hazards Poison novas, cold waves, Ancient Spires, Lower Resist, bone spells, damaging ground trails, Conjured Souls and Void Knights.
Entry effects Pre-buffs are removed and your mercenary is killed upon entering. Prepare to recast your buffs inside.
Re-entry Tier 0 provides three single-use portals in town. If you die, you can return through an unused portal. Entering grants temporary invulnerability while you recover your body. Re-entry is not possible in Tier 1 and Tier 2.
Exit You cannot freely leave while enemies remain. Clear each stage to progress through the encounter; otherwise, save and exit or die.
Guaranteed reward Tainted Worldstone Shard after defeating the final Rathma and Mendeln pair.

Final pair: Rathma and Mendeln share life. Damage dealt to either boss reduces their shared life pool; you do not need to defeat two independent life bars.

Fight progression

Unlike the Diablo-Clone and Lucion fights, the Rathma/Mendeln fight consists of three clearing stages alternating with three boss fights. Each of these boss fights have slightly different, but overlapping mechanics. Clear each stage and use its progression portal to reach the next part of the encounter.

Fight Overview

Stage Event What happens
1 Ruins of the Necropolis Travel through this area to get to the portal at the end. You will encounter a range of monsters, such as Burning Souls, Stygian Dolls and Zombies who will drop many needed potions. These mobs also have a chance at non-item drops, such as Trang-oul's Jawbone. It is worth noting that this first stage drops far less potions than the others.
2 Mendeln alone Mendeln uses his regular skills and attacks. Blood-golem events alternate with poison-cloud and long-duration poison-nova events. Ancient Spires add cold-projectile hazards.

5 Decayed Highlands Travel through this area to find the portal in one of three possible spawn places. Here the monsters are Void Knights, Hydras and Frogs.
6 Rathma and Mendeln together Both bosses attack simultaneously and share life. Rathma summons Void Knights; both bosses are immune while the knights remain alive. Mendeln retains his poison-event sequence and Lower Resist, but does not summon the healing blood golems.

Rewards

Defeating the final pair guarantees a Tainted Worldstone Shard, used to corrupt an Annihilus.

Lightning novas and positioning

  • Watch Lucion's movement toward the centre and the start of the nova animation.
  • The nova uses slow, weaker wave to allow you time to seek the right position and the fast lightning waves can be deadly.

Rewards

Defeating Lucion grants access to the Temple of the Triune. An amulet desecration can add a second corruption modifier, but success is not guaranteed.

Golden chests in the Temple of the Triune drop one additional item per difficulty tier.
