Prime Evils

Monsters with the Prime Evil tag all come with additional buffs. Prime Evil monsters include:

  • All act bosses, classic bosses and classic ubers
  • All map bosses
  • All dungeon monsters and bosses
  • All new ubers and their minions


The additional buffs that they gain are:

  • 200% damage to minions and mercenaries
  • Immune to slow effects (cold damage, Decrepify, Holy Freeze, Slows Target by X%, etc.)
  • Immune to Dim Vision, Terror, Confuse, and Attract
  • Immune to Sanctuary's physical resistance removal

Ubers

All ubers regenerate life like most monsters, but their high life means they regenerate substantially more per second than most. Monsters do not regenerate life while being damaged, so continuous damage such as Poison Damage or Open Wounds will halt their regeneration entirely. Prevent Monster Heal does not affect ubers. Note that ubers' immunities are not shown beneath their name like they are for other monsters.

Ubers within Uber Tristram no longer regenerate health

Uber Tristram Prime Evils

When the final prime evil is defeated, they drop a Hellfire Torch and a Black Soulstone.
  • Uber Tristram is now accessed with Pandemonium Talisman
  • All pre-buffs are removed when characters enter Uber Tristram
  • Players can no longer freely re-enter Uber Tristram while enemies remain within - when first entering the area, three single-use silver portals are created in town and the area may only be re-entered by using them (players get their original attempt plus three extra attempts)
  • Uber Mephisto uses level 20 Conviction (-50% resistances, was -125%)
  • Uber Baal now casts his own version of Lower Resist (-75% resistances, lasts about 25 seconds)
  • Uber Mephisto, Diablo, and Baal now have 10% more health and 25% less defense
  • These ubers no longer drop Standard of Heroes
Uber Diablo is sometimes referred to as "Pandemonium Diablo" with "Uber Diablo" being reserved for Diablo-Clone, but that nomenclature is avoided here.

Uber Ancients

  • New encounter featuring uber versions of Madawc, Talic, and Korlic that is accessed with Relic of the Ancients
  • Players can choose to fight 1, 2, or all 3 of the ancients by toggling their statues prior to starting the fight
  • When defeated, a Standard of Heroes will drop. If more than one ancient was fought, each additional ancient beyond the first adds a chance for another to drop.

Diablo-Clone

  • Diablo-Clone is now accessed with Vision of Terror
  • Diablo-Clone drops Annihilus when slain
  • If the Vision of Terror is empowered to difficulty 1 or 2, the fight will be more difficult and Diablo-Clone will have a 1/200 chance to drop an exclusive unique item (Overlord's Helm, Dark Abyss, Itherael's Path, Hadriel's Hand, Aidan's Scar)
  • Diablo-Clone is much stronger, and fighting him is substantially different
    • All pre-buffs are removed when characters enter Diablo-Clone's arena
    • Players cannot freely exit/re-enter the arena while Diablo-Clone lives (a portal is created when he is defeated)
    • Diablo-Clone becomes immune to all damage when players are more than ~10 yards from him
    • Meteors fall randomly throughout the fight
    • Bone Spirits appear from Trapped Souls throughout the fight and create dangerous patches of burning ground when colliding with players
    • The fight is divided into four phases
      • Between each phase, Diablo-Clone becomes temporarily immune to all damage and releases 6 especially deadly fire novas in succession
      • A horde of skeletal enemies (Bone Warriors, Skeleton Archers, Horror Mages) rise from the ground during each phase (60% poison resistance)
      • Skeletons cannot be cursed
      • Skeletons have 75% poison length reduction
      • Additional meteors and bone spirits are created in later phases
    • Diablo-Clone can no longer be chilled
    • Diablo-Clone can no longer be damaged by Static Field
    • Diablo-Clone now has more base health (10 530 HP) compared to the LOD-Version (6 427 HP).
    • Diablo-Clone now has 15% chance to block (was 50%)
    • Diablo-Clone now has 75% poison length reduction and 95% curse length reduction

Rathma and Mendeln

Rathma and Mendeln are two bosses part of an endgame boss-fight that can be accessed with Voidstone.

  • Rathma/Mendeln drops a Tainted Worldstone Shard when slain which can be used to corrupt Annihilus.
  • If the Voidstone is empowered to difficulty 1 or 2, the fight will be more difficult and Rathma will have 1/200 chance to drop an exclusive unique item (The Third Eye, Band of Skulls, Cage of the Unsullied) and also have a 1/50, 1/48, 1/46 chance to drop either Horadric Navigator, Horadric Almanac, or Skeleton Key.
  • Lower Resist from Mendeln (in both phase 1 and 3) scales per tier, starting at -25 at T0, -29 T1, and -33 at T2.
  • "Rathma" can refer to the actual boss, but is a colloquial term for this fight as a whole.
  • The fight consists of 3 pre-phases, alternating with 3 main phases. I.e. pre-phase 1, main phase 1, pre-phase 2 etc.
    • The pre-phases consist of a variety of mobs in three different terrains, mostly undead, who drop mana, full rejuvenation and antidote potions. It is also possible that the undead mobs in this fight can drop Trang-oul's Jawbone. It is worth noting that the first pre-phase drops far less potions than the others.
    • The three main phases are as follows: Mendeln alone, Rathma alone and finally both together. In each of these main phases, there are a variety of mechanics, and their cue is the boss health. For example, in phase 1 when Mendeln reaches a specific health level he will summon four blood golems. In phase 3, Mendeln and Rathma are health-linked: dealing damage to one deals damage to the other.
    • Almost all damage sources are against you in this fight, the one exception being lightning damage. The only source of lightning damage during the Rathma/Mendeln fight is in pre-phases, namely Burning Souls and Void Watchers. Mendeln's skills are mostly poison, with one cold skill, and the towers in his arena fire glacial spikes that do large amounts of cold damage. Rathma's skills are magic damage, and the void knights that he summons deal damage type corresponding to their right hand e.g. red = fire, blue = cold, green = poison and purple = physical. As in normal content, the void knights also have an array of curses they can use.

Rathma and Mendeln skills:

  • Rathma has a variety of magic damage spells
    • Bone Spear: with a burning trail that deals damage equaling a percentage of your life pool. This skill is unparryable by Weapon Block and Holy Sword, and unavoidable by Evade and Dodge
    • Teeth: like Teeth.
    • Bone nova: which creates a burning ring at maximum radius. (The Third Eye amulet gives this ability to players on casting)
    • Bone Spear plus teleport: Rathma teleports to your location and casts the above bone spear, he does this three times in quick succession and does not cast anything in between the three spears.
    • Summon Conjured Soul: Summons a skeleton mage that casts fissure, dealing fire damage in that area.
  • Mendeln has mostly poison skills, and one cold skill
    • Poison ball: This explodes on impact or maximum range, creating a mini poison nova
    • Lower resist: See above for -res amounts based on fight tier.
    • Sludge nova: Similar look to poison nova, but deals instant damage of the poison type, not damage over time.
    • Cold wave: Similar to Baal's cold wave

In addition to the above, both bosses have abilities that they cast when they reach a certain life percentage.

  • Rathma simply summons void knights, these knights vary in element and number.
    • At 70% health: 1 Cold, 2 Fire, 1 Poison, at 50% HP: 1 Cold, 1 Fire, 2 Poison, 1 Physical, and at 30% HP: 2 Cold, 2 Fire, 1 Poison, 1 Physical. While the knights are out Rathma continues casting his other abilities and is invulnerable to damage until they are all killed. When fighting Mendeln and Rathma together in the third main phase, both bosses are invulnerable while the knights are out.
  • Mendeln has two different phase events.
    • Blood golems: Mendeln teleports to the middle of the arena, and summons four blood golems. These march towards him, healing him if they reach him before dying. This occurs three times during the fight.
    • Vine: Mendeln teleports to the middle of the arena, and starts casting poison vines, releasing a poison cloud around the edge of the arena. At the end of this sequence he casts a deadly poison nova with exceedingly long poison duration. This occurs three times during the fight.
    • In addition to the above, there are four cold spires that cast a glacial spike that deals immense cold damage.
    • These phases alternate starting with blood golems. During these phases Mendeln is invulnerable to damage until the golems have disappeared or the poison nova has been cast. When fighting Mendeln and Rathma together in the third main phase, Mendeln does not summon the blood golems.

Lucion

  • Lucion is the new uber boss which can be accessed by using a Shadow of Hatred by the Blood Fountain in Harrogath
  • Lucion and all his minions are monster level 110.
  • Lucion is unfreezable.
  • Fight has 3 difficulties. You can set them by cycling the Shadow of Hatred in Horadric Cube.
  • You will lose all buffs upon entering the boss area.
  • Killing this boss gives access to Temple of the Triune, where you can "desecrate" an amulet in the Sacrificial altar, giving it second corruption modifier on top of regular one. For more info, see Desecration.
    • Desecration chances are 50% (tier 0) or 60% (tier 1 and tier 2)
    • Added “demonic cube” as a rare drop from lucion 1/75 (tier 1 and tier 2 only)
    • Golden chests in the temple of the triune drop +1 additional item per tier of difficulty
