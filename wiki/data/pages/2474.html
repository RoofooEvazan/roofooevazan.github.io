For a detailed explanation of how Energy Shield absorbs and multiplies damage, see below.

Energy Shield Mechanics & Examples
  • Energy Shield's Damage Absorption % (soft levels) is applied first, and determines how much of the incoming damage is "sent" to Life and Mana, respectively.
    • Damage still "sent" to Life then gets normal damage reductions (PDR/MDR/Resistances/Absorb/etc) applied.
  • Damage "sent" to Mana then has the Mana Loss Multiplier % (hard levels) applied.
  • The resulting number then drains or "damages" your Mana pool.
  • If the full amount of incoming damage would reduce Mana below 0, Energy Shield will only absorb damage equal to the amount that will drain the rest of your remaining Mana, and the excess will damage your Life unaffected by Energy Shield.
    • Energy shield stays active when out of mana in PD2.

Example 1: Level 40 Energy Shield (20 Hard Levels)

  • 100 Damage Taken
  • Energy Shield Absorbs 90% of that - 90 damage "sent" to Mana, 10 damage "sent" to Life
  • Mana Loss Multiplier applies to the 90 damage "sent" to Mana (87.5% at Hard Level 20)
    • 90 * 87.5% = 78.75
  • Mana is reduced/"damaged" by 78.75, Life is damaged by 10.
    • Other damage reductions will still apply to the Life damage.

Example 2: Level 6 Energy Shield (6 Hard Levels)

  • 100 Damage Taken
  • Energy Shield Absorbs 25% of that - 25 damage "sent" to Mana, 75 damage "sent" to Life
  • Mana Loss Multiplier applies to the 25 damage "sent" to Mana (175% at Hard Level 6)
    • 25 * 175% = 43.75
  • Mana is reduced/"damaged" by 43.75, Life is damaged by 75.
    • Other damage reductions will still apply to the Life damage.

Example 3: Level 40 Energy Shield (20 Hard Levels) but only 50 mana remaining

  • 100 Damage Taken
  • Energy Shield Absorbs 90% of that - 90 damage "sent" to Mana, 10 damage "sent" to Life
  • Mana Loss Multiplier applies to the 90 damage "sent" to Mana (87.5% at Hard Level 20)
    • 90 * 87.5% = 78.75 damage
  • 78.75 is more than current mana (50), so Energy Shield will only absorb (50 / 87.5%) = 57.143 damage.
  • Mana is reduced/"damaged" by 50, Life is damaged by 42.857.
    • Other damage reductions will still apply to the Life damage.

While Energy Shield is calculated before any damage reductions as described above, Mana gained from "Damage Taken Gained as Mana when Hit" applies only to Life lost after all reductions. See the Amazon Basin Wiki on Energy Shield and Damage Taken Goes to Mana for details.

Other Sources: Uldyssian's Awakening (Level 30), Tempest & Epiphany (Level 16)
