Welcome to the Project Diablo 2 Wiki!
Project Diablo 2 is a mod for and by passionate Diablo 2 fans.
We aim to maintain the Diablo II: Lord of Destruction experience and provide consistent ladder resets while improving on the game as if development never ceased.

Major updates and ladder resets are planned every 4-6 months.
Season 13: Betrayal began on April 24th, 2026 and will end on (approximately) October 18, 2026.
Season 14: Alliance will begin on October 23rd, 2026.

Anything not listed on this wiki should be unchanged from Lord of Destruction.
The information here is maintained by normal PD2 community members on a volunteer basis.

If you see errors or missing information on the wiki, please let others know on the #wiki-editing Discord channel, add it to the To Do page, or Create an Account and edit it yourself!
