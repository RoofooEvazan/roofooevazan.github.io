Frozen Orb's primary Orb lasts 1.8 seconds (45 frames) and travels 14 Yards. While traveling, it fires an Ice Bolt outward in a "spray" pattern every 0.04 seconds (every frame). Each Ice Bolt also has a range of 14 yards. The primary Orb does not deal damage, only the Ice Bolts do. When the Orb hits a wall or other impassable terrain, or at the end of its duration, the Orb explodes releasing 16 Ice Bolts in all directions. The explosion's Ice Bolts will not do damage if the Orb hit terrain, but they will if the Orb reached the end of its duration.

Frozen Orb has no Cooldown in Project Diablo 2, but it was changed to the "Slow Spell" Cast Rate.

Frozen Orb receives bonuses from:

  • Ice Bolt: +4% Damage per Level
  • Frost Nova: +4% Damage per Level
