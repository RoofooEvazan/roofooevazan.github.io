For a detailed explanation of Vengeance's mechanics, see below.

Vengeance Mechanics
Vengeance Damage
  • Vengeance deals Fire, Lightning, and Cold Damage. This damage is calculated from 3 sources in order:
  1. Weapon Damage
    • Vengeance converts 100% of most Weapon Damage into Elemental damage.
      • Included Damage: Physical weapon damage, Fire/Lightning/Cold damage from items (equipment and charms).
      • Excluded Damage: Poison damage, Magic damage (unconfirmed), Fire/Cold/Lightning Attack damage granted by spells (Paladin Auras and Cold/Fire Enchant).
        • Critical Damage, Crushing Blow, Life Leech, etc. are also excluded from Vengeance itself but since Vengeance is a weapon Attack, these attributes (and the above ones) will have a (small) effect on the normal weapon hit and melee splash.
    • This conversion is 1:1 for all 3 Elements, not split into 3 Elements.
      • For example, 100 weapon damage will turn into 100 Fire, 100 Cold, and 100 Lightning Damage, not 33 damage of each.
  2. Skill Damage
    • Vengeance's flat Elemental Skill Damage is then added to the Converted Damage for each Element.
    • The total Damage is then multiplied by the Skill Elemental Damage +%. The Synergy bonuses are added to the % here, and all Synergies apply to all three Damage types.
      • For example, the Holy Fire synergy adds up to 40% Damage to all three elements, not just Fire Damage.
  3. Mastery Damage
    • +% Elemental Skill Damage/Mastery then applies.
    • Each type of Mastery applies to each element individually.
      • For example, Level 6 Resist Fire Aura (or two 5% Fire Facets) will increase the Fire Damage of Vengeance by 10%, but not its Cold or Lightning Damage.
Vengeance Chaining
  • Vengeance does its damage in its radius, and any enemy hit by Vengeance has a chance to chain Vengeance, instantly repeating its effect and radius around that enemy.
  • Vengeance has no Next Hit Delay, meaning an enemy in range of multiple Vengeance radii can be hit by Vengeance multiple times.
  • This chaining will repeat until there are no more enemies to hit, or it reaches the maximum chain distance.

Vengeance Receives Bonuses From:

  • Holy Fire: +2% Elemental Damage per Level
  • Holy Freeze: +2% Elemental Damage per Level
  • Holy Shock: +2% Elemental Damage per Level
  • Conviction: +2% Elemental Damage per Level
