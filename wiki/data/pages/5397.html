GENERAL CHANGES

  • Added an option to hide game passwords in automap options.
  • Shared Stash:
    • Enabled shared stash in single player.
      • Note: this and the materials tab are not compatible with Plugy. If you still want to use Plugy you can run the game with the -plugy command line argument which will disable these features.
    • Added personal and shared gold functionality.
    • Updated the personal stash gold cap to 5 million.
    • Updated the player gold cap to 5 million (This is not based on level and is 5 million cap at all levels).
    • Updated the shared gold stash cap to 10 million.
    • Added hover descriptions to stash tabs.
    • Added stash tab hotkeys.
  • Added a Materials Stash Tab:
    • Materials deposited into your stash with Shift + Right Click will automatically be deposited to this tab regardless of what page you are on.
    • The tab allows rejuvenation potions to be stacked within it.
      • Note:
        • Shift + Right Click will prioritize filling your belt.
        • Shift + Ctrl + Right Click will fill your cube with rejuvenation potions.
    • The keybinds are as follows:
      • Right click to withdraw a stack of 1 to your inventory.
      • Left click to withdraw a stack of 1 to your mouse.
      • Ctrl + (Left / Right Click) to withdraw a stack of 5 to your (mouse / inventory).
      • Shift + (Left / Right Click) to withdraw a stack of 50 to your (mouse / inventory).
      • Ctrl + Shift + Right Click to Deposit a stack of 50 to your cube.
      • Ctrl + Shift + Left click to Deposit a single item to your cube.
        • Rejuvenation Potions are capped to +20.
      • The Materials Tab can be opened via mouse wheel, through the new Materials Tab Stash button, or with the keybind button (default X but configurable in options).

  • Added a new rare material drop the Demonic Cube:
    • Can be cubed with a set or unique to reroll the stats.
    • Maintains the sockets of a unique/set item as well as what was socketed within them.
    • Can be used on any item of unique/set rarity.
    • Cannot be used on a corrupted or mirrored item.
    • Drops off any monster in Hell or off Tier 1 and Tier 2 Lucion.
  • Added WASD Support.
    • Note: The WASD binds can be changed in the configuration menu
  • Added Controller Support:
    • The default controller configurations use "Twin Stick Shooter" style controls, using the left stick for movement and the right stick to aim.
    • The further you push the right stick the further the ability will be cast away from the player.
    • Directly supports PS4/PS5 and Xbox controllers.
    • Steam Deck™ supported with proper install (instructions will be posted).
    • Bluetooth PS5 controllers not supported.
    • Unsupported controllers can be used through Steam Input.
    • Seamless mouse and keyboard to controller transitioning on input, simply press a button and go then click or move your mouse to revert back!
    • Sensitivity and Aim Assist options available.
    • Quickcast can be enabled for a more "traditional" style of controls where the player presses a bind and it is immediately fired forward from the player instead of requiring aiming with the second joystick.
  • Added a setting to make allies abilities and summons appear translucent.
  • Added a new "Controls" menu that contains WASD and Controller settings.
  • Added an option in the settings menu to show life and mana values above the globes.
  • Lucion will be enabled in single player and non ladder.
  • Added a new single player settings menu with the following settings:
    • A /Players slider that will save between games & game launches.
    • An option to Regenerate Levels (Levels will be reset with new layouts on save and exit).
  • Changed worldstone shard text color from purple to red to properly match the corrupted tag.
  • Duriel now has his quest completion flag triggered from being killed instead of talking to Tyrael.
  • Shapeshift forms are now kept on act transition.
  • Depositing, withdrawing, and dropping gold will now always put the maximum gold amount in by default (i.e. no more typing).
  • You can now withdraw the max amount of gold.
    • Previously you had to withdraw an exact amount that was less than your gold cap, otherwise it would withdraw nothing.
  • Fortified kills now count as 2 kills in your .kills stats.
  • Monster Decrepify slow reduced from -50% to -30%.
  • Oblivion Knights:
    • Now have a chance to cast curses instead of always casting a curse on an uncursed player.

  • Added help text to the bottom of item tooltips, this will display available hotkeys.

TRADE WEBSITE

New "block user" feature:

  • You now have the ability to block other users to prevent them from messaging you and creating offers on your trades. You can block them in the "gear" menu on a chat you have with that user and also as an additional option on their offer on your trade. Once you've blocked them their names will be displayed in red when you view their trades to indicate that, while there's nothing that is stopping you from trading with them, communication will be hard since they won't be able to message you on the site. Users that you have blocked will show up in your settings page, and you can unblock users there if you wish.

Currency display:

  • With the awesome introduction of the currency/materials portion of the stash you can view this information on the site in your character and account pages. Currently you are unable to list items directly from this display like you can with your normal stash pages, but you could pull out a stack of the item into your normal stash and list it at that point.

New free-form profile info:

  • In your settings you can write profile info (markdown supported) for anything you want: personal rune price list, builds you're looking to gear up, etc.

Public stash pages:

  • In your settings you can mark certain stash pages as public, so that anyone can view them through the armory. Couples of notes with this: because of the way D2 was originally written we can't show this info on the character view screen, only the armory page. Also note that, if you have multiple game accounts linked to your site account, this setting will affect all of those game accounts.

Responsive listing layout:

  • Just added some responsive styles to take advantage of wider screens, moving the user info/actions to the right of the item info for wide views.

Timezone and contact preference settings:

  • You can set these in your settings and they will show up on your listing on the wider view only (the narrow view is too cluttered already, hence the move to the responsive version). Note here that daylight savings time is annoying so you may see things off by an hour, but it at least gives you an indication of whether the person not responding to you is most likely sleeping.
