Non-Equipment

  • General Items
    • Worldstone Shard (corrupts equipment/maps)
    • Larzuk's Puzzlebox (adds sockets to equipment)
    • Larzuk's Puzzlepiece (adds sockets to equipment - more common but cannot be used with uniques/sets)
    • Larzuk's Malus (adds 1 socket to equipment - can be purchased with gold)
    • Jewel Fragments (stackable "Jewel" ingredient)
    • Throwing Potions
      • Cold: Chilling Potion, Frost Potion, Freezing Potion
      • Lightning: Charged Potion, Static Potion, Shock Potion
    • Crafting Infusions (used to craft equipment - no extra gems/runes/jewels needed)
      • Blood Craft Infusion
      • Caster Craft Infusion
      • Hitpower Craft Infusion
      • Safety Craft Infusion
      • Vampiric Craft Infusion
      • Bountiful Craft Infusion
      • Brilliant Craft Infusion
    • Demonic Cube (uncommon drop from Lucion/anywhere, rerolls stats on a unique/set item while retaining items socketed in it)
    • Tainted Worldstone Shard (drops from Rathma, corrupts Annihilus)
    • Horadrim Navigator (uncommon drop from Rathma/anywhere, unlimited TP tome)
    • Horadrim Almanac (uncommon drop from Rathma/anywhere, unlimited ID tome which identifies all items simultaneously)
    • Skeleton Key (uncommon drop from Rathma/anywhere, unlimited key)
    • Vial of Lightsong (uncommon drop from anywhere, makes an item become ethereal)
    • Lilith's Mirror (uncommon drop from anywhere, duplicates an item)
  • Uber-Related Items
    • Relic of the Ancients (used to access Uber Ancients, made by combining one of each sigil)
      • Sigil of Madawc (from T1 maps)
      • Sigil of Talic (from T2 maps)
      • Sigil of Korlic (from T3 maps)
    • Pandemonium Talisman (used to access Uber Tristram, made by combining one of each uber organ)
    • Vision of Terror (used to access Diablo-Clone, made by combining Prime Evil Soul, Black Soulstone, & Pure Demonic Essence)
      • Prime Evil Soul (from Hell Diablo/Baal)
      • Black Soulstone (from Uber Tristram)
      • Pure Demonic Essence (from Map bosses)
    • Voidstone (used to access Rathma, made by combining Splinter of the Void, Trang-Oul's Jawbone, & Hellfire Torch)
      • Splinter of the Void (from Shadow of Mendeln map event)
      • Trang-Oul's Jawbone (from undead enemies)
      • Hellfire Ashes (stackable "Hellfire Torch" ingredient)
    • Shadow of Hatred (used to access Lucion, made by combining Demonic Insignia, Talisman of Transgression, Flesh of Malic)
      • Demonic Insignia
      • Talisman of Transgression
      • Flesh of Malic
  • PvP-Related Items
    • Moor Duel Arena (used to access a "Blood Moor" themed PvP zone)
    • Desert Duel Arena (used to access a "Desert" themed PvP zone)
    • Dueling Mana Potion (standard potions are disabled in PvP arenas)
