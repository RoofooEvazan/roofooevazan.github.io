Viability / Purpose of the Build

Pretty much the best early necromancer travincal farmer with little to no gear requirements.

Watch the Travincal Farm video

Skills

See picture for skills, basically go for 20 Golem Mastery and 20 Clay Golem. That is enough for Travincal. Suggestions to add are 10 Curse Mastery and 1pt in Amplify Damage, Decrypify and Lower Resist. For speed you max Bloodwarp. Good additions are Revives or max Amplify Damage.

Attributes

Get as much str/dex as you need for gear, the rest into vitality. For instance get 95str for War Traveller boots and 38dex for Wizspike.

Gear

For summons its basically all about the +skills so get as many as you can.

Early Game Gear

Shop for +3 summon wand, gamble for +3 summon amulet, find a +3 summon necro head. Can also make a splendor in necro head with +3 Clay Golem. Make a Lore helmet for another +1 and start gambling circlets for +3. For summons its basically all about the +skills so get as many as you can.

Mid Game Gear

My favourite outfit for Travincal is Wizard Spike + Kalans + Hustle Runeword. Vigor, Burst of Speed and a ton of resist.
Other options to speed things up are Sanders Boots and Peasant Crown. For MF add Chanceguards, Goldwrap and a Gheeds charm.

Mercenary

For merc get an A3 cold merc with a spirit paladin shield, neophyte sceptre (with prayer if possible), stealth runeword armor and random 10fcr belt/gloves to at least hit 86fcr on him. He will deal a lot of damage and the cold damage will slow monsters to make it safer.

Leveling

Get a dagger with poison strike and stab things until level 6 while saving your points until level 12, start maxing Golem Mastery and put the saved points into Clay Golem. After that and let them do the work.
This build works for SC and HC, for HC it's recommend going the Blood Golem route for the life on hit.

Farming

You farm Travincal, but you are very good at it!
While farming look out for gear that makes faster builds possible, Bonespear for Chaos or Corpse Explosion in T1 maps.
