A guide for a Werebear using Shock Wave. Think of it as a sturdy, but slow moving furry sorc casting multi shot.

Shock wave has about the range and spread of multi shot clearing a quater of the screen per cast. High defense, high life, stuns and a castable on demand blocker (Summon Grizzly) means he barely takes any damage. On the downside he has limited scaling options, can not use any form of teleport and thus has to drag his hairy ass everywhere

This is a mapping and LoD content build. The dps to comfortabel do any ubers is to low. The great aoe of shock wave is meant to clear trash.

pros:

  • a great and save mapper with lots of defense and sustain
  • The defense scaling of werebear is great against trash mobs, but not so much against bosses
  • very good sustain via Oak Sage and Carrion Vine
  • high min damage of shock wave means consistent damage
  • very forgiving gearing, just throw any combination of the listed items at him and he will work

cons:

  • although min damage is is high, he has limited damage scaling
  • has to walk everywhere and teleports do not work in bear from

Mechanics

Shockwave is a spell dealing physical damage and - same as any other spell - its damage is scaled by:

  • +skills
  • fast cast rate (fcr)
  • +x% damage of its damage type and -x% to enemy resistances.
    • There are no sources of +x% physical damage in the game and limited sources of -x% to enemy physical resistances

Physical damage builds dont have facets, but comparably easy acces to resistance penetration (via Amplified damge and Battle Cry), which means they are easy and cheap to build.

notably its damage is NOT scaled by:

  • enhanced damage on items (e.g. Fortitude (Chest))
  • weapon damage

Werebear as a caster benefits from:

  • defense
  • flat life / flat vit via Lycanthropy

Note that the increased damage of werebear is wasted on shock wave (it is enhanced damage). Additionally the shock wave projectiles can not shot gun.

We can draw a handful of conclusions from his Breakpoints:

  • Faster Cast Rate (FCR) going up one frame is about 10% more DPS, which is about the same as +2 skill points. So when in doubt take +1skills over fcr, but aiming for 11 Frames per Animation (fpa) is reasonable. Notable breakpoints: 40/63/99/163
  • Faster Block Rate (FBR) Breakpoints in werebear form got buffed compared to vanilla, but are still among the worst in the game. Thus i recommend to do what melee druids do and use a 2-hand cast weapon, a stave.

Skills

20 pts in main skill, prerequisits and synergies:

  • Shock wave
  • Werebear
  • Twister (unfortunately useless otherwise)

1-20 pts in utility skills:

  • Summon Grizzly gets a second grizzly at 20 hard points
  • Oak Sage and Carrion Vine are synergies to one another. they give max life and sustain
  • Lycanthropy for more life
  • (maybe) Hunger for move speed, but is a pain to keep up
  • (maybe) Hurricane to freeze nearby enemies, but is a pain to keep up and feels useless with the shock wave stuns

Base of the build is done at around lvl 75. Everything else is bonus

Attributes

  • strength to wear gear might be above 200, but can also be very low with caster gear
  • dex enough for stave of choice or max block, if you really want to
  • vitality rest
  • if you feel like doing something unconventionell/ineffectiv you can use Rain and put points here to boost the Cyclone Armor proc

Gear

This is a bread and butter caster: aim for 100fcr, as many +skills as possible, max resist and a source of can not be frozen (cnbf). When in doubt get a rare item with as many of these as possible and you are fine.

Mercenary

Mostly used to reduce physical resistance of enemies. Skills that reduce physical resistance are Amplify Damage (AD) and Battle Cry. AD can be procced by act1, act2 and act5 mercs, via their weapon slot.

The armor slot is based used by Heavenly Garb. It adds Sanctuary (Skill), which reduces undead physical resistance to 0.

Fill the remaining gear slots with cnbf and life leech. Add pdr, life and some resists

Leveling

If you want to level as shock wave go werebear -> shockwave -> werebear -> twister. Use maul until shockwave is avialable.

Id rather just level as a fire caster or summon and respecc around the end of nightmare/beginning of hell at around lvl75

Tips

Put shock wave on right click. Right click on the screen. If you click further, the spread becomes tighter, if you click closer it becomes thinner, but tighter on the edges.

I usually use a medium distance, such that the shock wave projectiles barely touch. This yields best coverage without "wasting" projectiles because of the shot-gun limitation

If you are running LoD content put a point in gust and use najs off-hand to reach your destination

Where to farm

LoD content is easy. Cows, CS aLvl85 zones and corrupted zones work great. Avoid zones with swarms, e.g. far oasis. Baal runs are okish, we have to walk wstlvl2 and 3 and baal takes a little time, hexes are phys immune. Maps T1-T3 are great, just avoid tomb of zultan because of the phys immune scarabs.
