The given reason is: we solved it


On May 28th 2025, SenpaiSomething posted a riddle alongside the patch notes for the first post-release patch of Season 11 on Discord.

Rumors of an otherwordly relic, hidden inside of an indestructible but otherwise unremarkable blade, have reached the Rogue Encampment. Perhaps a magical smithing hammer could have the power to reveal the weapon?

Currently, there is no official confirmation that the riddle has been solved, though some have reported solving it by showing screenshots of the otherwordly relic.

Spoilers


Speculation ensued and people eventually supposedly started finding the rumored otherwordly relic, which is a unique Phase Blade named Armageddon's Blade.

It appears the solution is to take a normal Phase Blade and having Charsi imbue it. It has a RNG chance of imbuing into Armageddon's Blade. This is not confirmed as of yet, but some item filters suggest picking up Phase Blades for this purpose.

Rumor has it that it's about a 1 in 70 chance (1.43% probability).

An interesting detail is that it seemingly becomes a two-handed weapon, despite the fact that Phase Blade is usually a one-handed sword. In fact, the sword uses an entirely new custom two-handed Crystal Sword base that only the class Paladin can equip and use.
