Project Diablo 2 Season 13 Betrayal Patch Notes

General Changes

  • Repair costs of a singular item now cap at 250k
  • You may now use potions during any mobility skills (E.g. charge, leap, whirlwind, etc)
  • All sources of critical strike are now combined instead of there being Critical Strike from Weapon Mastery and Critical Strike (amazon skill) as two independent rolls
  • All sources of critical strike now have a 2x damage multiplier (increased from 1.5x)
    • Note: This relates to “critical strike” not “deadly strike”
  • Increased the density of the act 5 portals in hell mode by roughly 67%
  • Doubled the drop rate of demonic cubes in hell mode
  • Lower density corrupted zones will now receive density increases when corrupted
    • Note: These numbers still need to be tuned during beta so zones will currently be a bit all over
  • Worldstone shard and map drop rates in corrupted zones have been reduced by roughly 50% to offset the density increases
    • Note: We actually plan to increase the drop rate a little bit from what is listed above
  • Normal weapon bases will now have a smaller splash radius compared to exceptional / elite
    • Note: When upgraded they will have the full melee splash radius
  • Items dropped from objects (chests, barrels, etc.) in corrupted zones or maps with a +Area Level bonus will now drop with the correct item level
  • Added a controller button graphics override setting
  • Freezes target no longer applies to melee splash
  • Ctrl-v paste now works in-game
  • Pet and mercenary health updates now happen every 15 frames instead of 30
    • Note: There are 25 frames per second in diablo 2
  • Increased the maximum number of items shown on the ground from 32 to 48
  • Item boxes will no longer cover the stash
  • Added a .age command
    • For now, any previously created characters won’t have an age and will just show “Ancient”.
    • This may be something we can fix for old characters in the future
  • Added a few more searchable sections to the stash search:
    • Weapon details: E.g. "Staff Class - Fast Attack Speed"
    • Item Description: E.g. "Tier: 2" on maps. "Cube with an item to add sockets" on a Puzzlebox
    • Socket Fillers: bonus stat details on unstacked runes and gems
  • Changed the way quick cast handles multiple spell casts in a short time frame (there should be less delay between casts)
    • This also extends to controller
    • Added a small input buffer to allow multiple casts even when not timed perfectly
  • Crushing blow damage scaling buff
    • Prime evil Crushing Blow base damage increased from 1/(80 + missing_hp^2) to 1/(70 + missing_hp*10)
    • Map bosses now use a base Crushing Blow damage of 1/30hp instead of the prime evil formula
    • Player count modifier for Crushing Blow damage reduced heavily:
      • Originally the player count hp% increase was added, which is 490% in p8
      • Originally went from 1/8hp in p1 to 1/498hp(~0.2% hp) in p8
      • Now uses player count hp% / 50
      • New range goes from 1/7hp in p1 to 1/(7+(490/50)) = 1/16.8(~6% hp) in p8
      • Ranged penalty was added back and applies 1.5x after all of the above changes, i.e. base damage is 1/(7*1.5)
  • Character Sheet
    • Changed the wording of the Defense hover string to match the Attack Rating one
    • Added melee defense and missile defense details to the hover display
      • It will only show both if they aren't the same value
    • Added Ignore Target's Defense, -% Target Defense, Attack Rating against Demons, and Attack Rating against Undead into the chance to hit calc hover display
      • Ignore Target's Defense and -% Target Defense will also take the target monster’s “quality” into account, so Ignore Target's Defense won’t be factored in when you’re fighting a unique/boss monster, for example.
    • Both of these hover detail updates will now also show super unique monster names
      • "Rakanishu" instead of "Fallen"
  • Sound Updates
    • Sounds are no longer added to the queue if their respective sound group is muted
      • Previously, sounds would get added to the queue and would go through the full cycle of sorting/prioritization, volume adjustment, playback, etc. even if the sound would never actually be heard
      • Now if you have Monster sounds muted, for example, those sounds just get dropped
    • Increased sound cache size from 5MB to 50MB
      • The previous cache was hilariously small.
      • Just loading into the game was taking up 35% of it, and walking around the cow level would max it out almost immediately.
      • Sounds would get loaded and unloaded pretty much constantly, even in a single player game (I’m sure it would be even worse for 8 player games)
    • Increased the number of channels from 16 to 32
    • Fixed a bug which would mute the music when the main sound slider was muted
    • Fixed a crash on act cutscene transition when using the -ns command line arg
    • Other minor performance optimizations
  • Did not add a secret
