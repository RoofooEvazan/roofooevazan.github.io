Added in season 10: Desecration, this mechanic allows to "desecrate" an amulet, giving it another corruption modifier on top regular corruption modifier. The amulet will also change it's appearance.

The corruption modifiers pool is only the high rarity amulet corruptions.

High-Rarity Amulet Corruption Mods
+1 to All Skills
+10% Faster Cast Rate
+10% Faster Run/Walk
+10% Curse Resistance
+[30-40]% Enhanced Damage
Cannot Be Frozen
+2% to ALL Maximum Resistances
All Resistances +[7-10]

Desecration is possible after killing uberboss Lucion, which was also added in season 10: Desecration.

WARNING: This action can brick the amulet, turning it into rare. The bricked amulet will, however, still retain two corruption mods, chosen at random.

The chance for a successful desecration depends on the tier chosen for Lucion

  • Tier 0: 50%
  • Tier 1: 60%
  • Tier 2: 60%
