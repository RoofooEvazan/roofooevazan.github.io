The following are cosmetic effects in Project Diablo 2.

These are visual effects only and have no mechanical game impact.

Character Aura Effects

  • Character Aura Effects, if unlocked, are activated in the "PD2 Options" section of the Esc menu. (Online Only)
  • Premium Auras are Account-wide, Online Only, and expire at the end of the Season they are earned.
  • Non-Premium Auras are attached to the character that unlocks them and will persist (on non-ladder) past the end of the Season they are earned.
    • Auras are not normally unlocked at all in single player, but Non-Premium Auras can be activated by editing config files and/or a character's Save file. (See this Discord thread for more information)

Alternate Item Skins

Alternative Item Skins are visual reskins of specific unique items. These Alternative Skins have new inventory artwork and a different "armor color" that changes the item's appearance on the Player Character when the item is equipped (for applicable item slots - Helms, Chest Armor, Shields, and Weapons).

All Item Alternative Skins have a very low chance to drop at any time when the eligible unique drops. The specific drop chances vary per item and are not publicly disclosed, but the chance of the Alternative Skin dropping is generally inverse to the overall rarity of the Unique Item (more common Unique Items have more rare Alternative Skins and vice-versa).

"Normal" and "Alternate" colors below refer to the colors that appear on the Player Character when the item is equipped; changed inventory artwork is shown on this page.
