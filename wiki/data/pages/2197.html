This page lists player-vs-player changes relative to the vanilla game. All changes mentioned here only apply with the PvP arenas unless otherwise noted.

PvP Tournaments

Each season there is typically at least one official PvP tournament for Softcore and Hardcore respectively for both High Level Dueling (HLD) and Low Level Dueling (LLD).

In these tournaments, High Levels (1-99) duel in Hell Difficulty while Low Levels (30) typically duel in Normal Difficulty.

The reward for winning an official tournament is an exclusive account-wide aura, similar to those rewarded to the first 3 level 99 characters per class and/or the first 3 boss kills for the uber bosses per class. If there are 16 or more participants per respective tournament then the top 3 placing accounts each are rewarded this aura.

Beyond these official tournaments there are also community PvP tournaments with large prize pools that are hosted frequently for both HLD and LLD characters throughout each Season. Some of these tournaments are arranged as Team vs Team (TvT) duels (a popular way to play for LLD). To inquire about community tournaments, and for any general PvP-related questions, discussion can be found in the respective HLD PvP or LLD PvP channels in the official Project Diablo 2 Discord.

General Changes

  • New dueling arenas are accessible via 'maps' which can be purchased from Akara in Act 1
  • Vendors within PvP arenas sell 'Dueling Mana Potions' (other potions cannot be used in the arenas)
  • Vendors within PvP arenas also sell a variety of 'Dueling Charms' tailored for both HLD and LLD characters:
    Grand Charms: +1 Skill Tree with 15 Life
    Large Charms: 2% Damage with 15 Life
    Small Charms: 20 Life, 15 Life, 10 Life + 2 Max Damage + 10 Attack Rating, 10 Life + 3% all resistances, 10 Life + 7% of one resistance, or 5% FHR + 7% of one resistance
    Note: These 'Dueling Charms' only work inside of the PvP arenas. Outside of the arenas their stats do not apply to the character.
  • Spectator mode can be toggled in the arenas by typing .spec or .spectate within the safe zone of an arena
  • After dying in these arenas no gold is dropped and characters revive without needing to retrieve their corpse
Note: Hardcore characters revive in the same way - Deaths are only 'safe' inside the arenas, while outside of the arenas deaths are permanent
  • The typical resistance penalties in Nightmare and Hell do not apply in the PvP arenas
  • Maximum resistances are capped based on the difficulty: 75% (Normal), 80% (Nightmare and Hell)
  • Pierce from passive sources (Equipment, Facets, Sorceress Masteries, etc.) does not work in the arena, this includes Physical pierce
Note: Skills that 'actively' apply a pierce 'debuff' such as Inferno, Arctic Blast, Lower Resist, Conviction, and/or Battle Cry still apply those -resistance debuffs
  • Open Wounds is limited based on the difficulty - it deals 8%/6%/4% of its damage in Normal/Nightmare/Hell
  • Stuns in PvP can no longer be reapplied to players who are already stunned, and attacks/hits/spells against stunned players will reduce the stun duration by 2 frames per hit
  • Regenerating charges (e.g. Blink on Enigma) regenerate 200% faster
  • Slow, Knockback (from items), Absorb, Attackers Take Damage, Life Leech, and Melee Splash are disabled
  • Player summons have their own "PvP modifier", dictating how much damage they take, of "85%".
  • Flat magic and physical damage reduction is capped at +25
  • Life replenish is capped at +30

Skill Differences

  • Dragon Flight: doesn't auto-aim
  • Mind Blast: stun lasts 1.5 seconds instead of 2 seconds, radius doesn't increase, doesn't name-lock
  • Whirlwind: doesn't have a dual-wield attack frame penalty and weapon range is capped at 3
  • Battle Cry: duration is increased by 5 seconds
  • Double Throw: doesn't bounce
  • Oak Sage: doesn't grant life replenish
  • Bone Spear: only fires 1 projectile, reduced projectile size (3 -> 2)
  • Holy Bolt: only fires 1 projectile
  • Smite: stun lasts up to 2.5 seconds instead of up to 10 seconds
  • Fist of the Heavens: doesn't auto-aim or name-lock
  • Charge: uses normal attack speed frames in melee range
  • Joust: doesn't auto-aim
  • Lightning: reduced projectile size (3 -> 2)
  • Teleport: doesn't have a 1-second damage debuff (Normal only)
  • Meteor: reduced explosion radius
  • Energy Shield: while active, Dueling Mana Potions cannot be used
  • Shadow Warrior and Shadow Master have additional modifiers (penalties) on top of the PvP damage modifier per difficulty.
  • Chance to parry or dodge is halved (Weapon Block, Holy Sword, Dodge, Evade)
  • Movement skills don't name-lock (Whirlwind, Leap, Leap Attack, Charge)
  • Healing skills (Holy Bolt, Holy Nova, Holy Light) don't heal allies

Known Bugs

  • Occasionally when interacting with the books within a PvP arena you may enter a state where you cannot target anything. You have to rejoin the game to fix this.
  • While in the Moor Duel Arena, if someone trades with Akara in Rogue Encampment it can cause the PvP vendor version of Akara to have the same items for trade as the one in Rogue Encampment. Unfortunately, the quickest solution to this is to create a new game.
