Number of Revives can be increased further with Blood Raven's Charge, Sacred Totem, and the Eternity Runeword.

Revives retain most of their Attributes, Skills and AI behavior they had as enemies, and thus have an extremely wide variety of potential stats, skills, & damage.

Notable exclusions to Revive abilities are any Skills that Buff or Heal, and Skills that spawn or resurrect other creatures.

Revives have +50% Velocity.

Revive Receives Bonuses From:

  • Desecrate: +20% Physical Damage per Level
  • Desecrate: +10% Elemental Damage per Level
