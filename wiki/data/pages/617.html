General

  • Ethereal bonus reduced from 50% to 25% (most exceptional/elite weapons rebalanced so that eth stats remain the same)
  • Ethereal items no longer have half durability
  • Strength and Dexterity requirements of ethereal items are reduced by 10 each
  • Throwing weapons (including potions) and arrows/bolts have unlimited quantity
  • All claws can now have pointmods (staffmods) - previously, claws couldn't if they had a qlvl below 40: all normal claws, Quhab, Wrist Spike, Fascia
  • Clubs can now have Druid pointmods
  • Daggers can now have pointmods for dagger-related skills (Poison Strike and most martial arts skills)
  • Quivers can now be Magic, Rare, and Unique
  • Quivers can now roll 1-2 sockets
  • Quivers now have Normal, Exceptional, and Elite versions
  • Quivers now have automods

Automods

Modifiers that automatically appear on all regular/magic/rare/crafted versions of items, and often unique/set versions too

Weapons

  • Melee weapons now have Melee Attacks Deal Splash Damage (except wands)
  • Staves now have 10%/30%/50% Faster Cast Rate (normal/exceptional/elite)
  • Daggers now have 20% Deadly Strike
  • Throwing Knives have 15% Critical Strike Multiplier

Quivers

  • Arrows now have +2/+10/+18 to Maximum Damage (normal/exceptional/elite)
  • Bolts now have -2%/-4%/-6% to Enemy Physical Resistance (normal/exceptional/elite)

Armor

  • Removed hidden -5%/-10% movespeed penalty from medium/heavy chests and shields
  • Paladin shields have reduced ranges for "All Resistances" automod
    • Rainbow reduced from +[8-15] to +[5-10]
    • Scintillating reduced from +[16-30] to +[10-15]
    • Prismatic reduced from +[25-35] to +[15-20]
    • Chromatic reduced from +[36-45] to +[20-30]
  • Spiked Shield, Barbed Shield, and Blade Barrier have Attackers take [2-4]/[16-20]/[80-100] Damage
  • Bone Shield, Grim Shield, and Troll Nest have Magic Damage Reduced by [2-4]/[4-6]/[6-8]

Damage

Damage Bonus from Strength/Dexterity

  • Polearms, Non-Class Spears, & One-Handed Swords now gain bonus damage equal to 75% str & 75% dex instead of 100% str. This doesn't include Two-Handed swords that are wielded one-handed.
  • Staves & Two-Handed Axes/Swords now gain bonus damage equal to 110% str instead of 100% str (same as Hammers)
  • Bows/Crossbows now gain bonus damage equal to 50% dex instead of 100% dex
  • Boots now gain bonus damage equal to 80% str instead of 120% str and kicks also benefit roughly twice as much from dexterity (now dex/3 - dex/2, was dex/6 - dex/5)

For example: A Bow gets +5% Enhanced Damage if you get +10 to Dexterity.

Base Damage

  • Normal weapon damage increased by ~4% on average
  • Exceptional weapon damage increased by ~42% on average to be closer to elite weapons and counteract the ethereal bonus being changed from 1.5x to 1.25x
  • Elite weapon damage increased by ~24% on average to counteract the ethereal bonus being changed from 1.5x to 1.25x, along with a few relatively minor adjustments for specific bases
  • Kick damage of boots adjusted
  • In Season 11 bow base damage was increased by 20% (30% for Matriarchal). Lesser basses haven't yet been updated to reflect this, only elite. Percentage increase from LoD to S11 also hasn't been updated.
