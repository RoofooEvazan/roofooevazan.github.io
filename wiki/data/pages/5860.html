Guilds

The Guild feature was added in Season 14 and is based on features that Blizzard North planned but never finished or released.

Guild membership is per character, not per account, so characters on the same account can belong to different guilds. A guild can have up to 500 members (characters), and up to 100 players can visit the Guild Hall at the same time. Softcore and Hardcore characters can join the same guild. Members can hold different ranks, each granting different permissions.

A Guild can have different tiers, granting different features. Softcore and Hardcore use separate ranks and have a separate Guild Vault.

Guild Hall

The Guild Hall is a place where members can come together, it is a small area set in an Act 1 environment and contains a house differing in size and construction based on the guild's tier. The guild hall can be joined by clicking the "Guild" button in the lobby and selecting "Enter Guild Hall". It can hold up to 100 players and is separated for Softcore and Hardcore characters.

Guild Vault

The Guild Vault allows members to share items they find (similar to the account-wide shared stash). When the vault is first unlocked it only has one tab; others are added by the following guild tier upgrades.

By default, every member (including those with the rank “Initiate”) can add items to the tabs and take items from them. Guild officers and the guild master can change this: right-clicking a vault tab header cycles through “Everyone” (grey, no highlight), “Members and above” (yellow highlight), “Officers and above” (blue highlight), and “Guild master only” (red highlight; can be enabled or disabled only by the guild master).

Softcore and Hardcore use a different vault (if enabled by reaching tier 3 on both).

Tiers

Upgrades require the members to donate gold; it can only be donated from the gold carried by the character, so only 5M gold can be donated at once. Gold accumulates, so a tier's mentioned cost is already partly paid by previous upgrades. Upgrades are automatic: As soon as the required gold amount is reached and open a portal to the upgraded guild hall.

A guild has separate ranks on Softcore and Hardcore, so each must be upgraded individually.
