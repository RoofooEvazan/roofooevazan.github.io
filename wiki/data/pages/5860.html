Guilds

The Guild feature was added in Season 14 and is based on features that Blizzard North planned but never finished or released.

Guild membership is per character, not per account, so characters on the same account can belong to different Guilds. A Guild can have up to 500 members (characters), and up to 100 players can visit the Guild Hall at the same time. Softcore and Hardcore characters can join the same guild. Members can hold different Ranks, each granting different permissions.

A Guild can have different tiers, granting different features. Softcore and Hardcore use separate tiers and have a separate Guild Vault and Trophy Case.

Guild Chat messages are not separated for Softcore/Hardcore, everyone online member receives them. Guild Chat can currently not be muted and passes through an activated /dnd mode.

Members and Ranks

To become a member of a Guild you need to be invited with your character name by an Officer or the Guild Master. A character that is already member of another Guild cannot be invited. But a character can have multiple open invitations.

Characters that accept a Guild invitation join as "Initiate". They can be then promoted to Warrior and then Officer. Members can be managed from the Steeg Stone in the Guild Hall, from the lobby's Guild menu or via chat commands by the Guild Master and Officers. Officers can only remove & invite, only the Guild Master can promote & demote.

There can be only one Guild Master per Guild. You can only own one Guild per account. The Guild Master character cannot be deleted, transfer the ownership first. The new owner can be any current guild member of any rank.

Hardcore: If the Guild Master character dies, he can still manage the guild from the lobby or via chat commands, he just can't enter the Guild Hall. Use the "Recreate Character" button in the character selection to create a new character and retain guild ownership.

Guild Hall

The Guild Hall is a place where members can come together, it is a small area set in an Act 1 environment and contains a house (or a glorious castle when upgraded) differing in size and construction based on the Guild's tier. The Guild Hall can be joined by clicking the "Guild" button in the lobby and selecting "Enter Guild Hall". It can hold up to 100 players and is separated for Softcore and Hardcore characters. Skills, Combat and Summons are disabled while in the Guild Hall.

The Guild Hall contains the Steeg Stone which allows Guild and member management and donations to upgrade the guild.

You can access your personal stash here and if unlocked use different vendors, read the Message Board, marvel at the items in the Trophy Case or use the Guild Vault

Steeg Stone

Clicking the Steeg Stone show information about the Guild and gives access to multiple actions:

  1. Displays the Guild's name and master.
  2. Shows the Guild's Charter and the Guild's website, both can only be edited by the Guild Master.
  3. Shows the list of members, those using a different mode (Softcore or Hardcore, Ladder or Non-Ladder) than you are displayed in grey. Clicking the column headers allows sorting the list. Selecting a member allows to remove it from the Guild (Officers and Guild Master only) or modifying its rank (Guild Master only)
  4. Shows the current Guild's Emblem, once it's unlocked at tier 2. It can be configured by the Guild Master.
  5. Shows the current amount of gold donated to the guild with a "Donate" button and the Guild's next tier, with the total gold required to unlock it. Hovering the tier shows the next tier's features.

Guild Vault

The Guild Vault allows members to share items they find (similar to the account-wide shared stash). When the vault is first unlocked it only has one tab; others are added by the following guild tier upgrades.

By default, every member can add items to the tabs and take items from them. Officers and the Guild Master can change this: right-clicking a vault tab header cycles through “Everyone” (grey, no highlight), “Members and above” (yellow highlight), “Officers and above” (blue highlight), and “Guild Master only” (red highlight; can be enabled or disabled only by the Guild Master). The Guild Vault can also hold more gold (>25M) than the shared account stash.

Softcore and Hardcore use a different vault (if enabled by reaching tier 3 on both).

Message Board

The Message Board - once unlocked - allows to leave messages for your fellow Guild members. It is shared for Softcore and Hardcore. All members can add new messages, old messages get removed when the screen is already filled.

Trophy Case

The Trophy Case - once unlocked - allows to show off the best items you found to your fellow Guild members. It is separate for Softcore and Hardcore and has 6 slots. Items added remain in you possession, the Trophy Case only contains a copy. All members can add new items to it or remove them. On removal you don't get the item back.

Tiers

Upgrades require the members to donate gold; it can only be donated from the gold carried by the character, so only 5M gold can be donated at once. Gold accumulates, so a tier's mentioned cost is already partly paid by previous upgrades. Upgrades are automatic: As soon as the required gold amount is reached the tier updates and opens a portal to the upgraded Guild Hall.

A Guild has separate tiers on Softcore and Hardcore, so each must be upgraded individually.
