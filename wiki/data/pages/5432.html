The following are skills in Project Diablo 2 used by Mercenaries.

Many of these skills are distinct versions of Player Skills balanced independently. Skills with distinct Mercenary versions will be denoted as such and have more detailed information.

For more information about Mercenaries, including PD2 changes, stats, equipment, etc., see: Mercenaries

General Information

Skill Info

  • Mercenaries have "Hard Levels" in their Skills, so they will gain those effects.
  • Mercenaries can also get synergies on their Skills, in the same way players do.
    • This applies to both their "natural" Skills and Skills from items they equip, if the Skill granting the synergy is the exact skill (i.e., not a Mercenary-only skill).
    • See below for synergies Mercenaries get from their "natural" Skills.
    • Mercenary Skills will also grant synergies to Skills from items. For example, an Act 2 Offensive mercenary wearing Doom will grant (up to) +198% Cold Damage on the Holy Freeze from his (up to) Hard Level 18 Blessed Aim.
    • Since Mercenaries get 'Hard' levels past 20, unlike players, synergy bonuses can be even higher than players get.
  • Skills and Auras have minimum level requirements, so mercenaries won't be able to use some skills at lower levels.
  • Mercenaries will benefit from the following +Skill bonuses:
    • +X to All Skills
    • +X to non-class Fire/Cold/Lightning/Magic Skills (as applicable to a skill)
    • +X to specific Skills if they use the Skill (e.g. a Barbarian helmet with +3 to Bash for an Act 5 Combat Mercenary, or a +3 Prayer Scepter for an Act 3 Cold Mercenary). The (Class Only) tag does not apply as long as the skill is an exact match.
      • Specific skill bonuses will only work if the skill is the exact same skill Players use (i.e., not a Mercenary-only skill).
  • Mercenaries will not benefit from:
    • +X to Class Skills (not even Act 5 Barbarians)
    • +X to Class Skill Trees (e.g. +X to Offensive Auras or +X to Combat Skills)
  • Each time mercenaries attack or use a skill, they choose which skill to use at random based on set probabilities.
    • For more information see Below: (Act 1, Act 2, Act 3, Act 4, Act 5)
    • Passive Skills and Auras are always active, and thus are not listed.

  • Mercenary Auras are always active as long as the level requirement has been met.
    • The level requirement for each Mercenary Aura is the same as for the corresponding Paladin Skill.
  • The difficulty in which a Mercenary is hired no longer affects the Aura the Mercenary has.
  • Mercenaries' native Auras and Auras granted from their items interact the same way as they do on players, meaning mercenaries can have multiple auras from skills and items, but an Aura granted by an item will not stack with an identical native Aura.
    • For example, an Act 1 Fire Mercenary's Vigor Aura will not benefit from wearing Harmony - the skill Aura will override the item Aura.

Merc Static Field can break Lightning Immunity. See Reducing Resistances for more information.

Merc Static Field's 25% Life reduction is Lightning Damage, reduced (or increased) normally by enemy Lightning Resistance.

Because the Life check is at the beginning of the damage calculation, Static Field's total damage can exceed the 55/70/85 cap. (see example below)

Merc Static Field Damage Example

Level 12 Merc Static Field (-32%) is cast against an Enemy with 50% Lightning Resistance in Hell difficulty

Cast 1: 12.5% Damage (25% * 50% Resistance)

Enemy reduced to 87.5% HP and 18% Lightning Resistance

Cast 2: 17.9375% Damage ((25% of 87.5% or 21.875%) * 18% Resistance)

Enemy reduced to 69.5625% Life and still at 18% Lightning Resistance

Cast 3: Enemy is below 85% Life, damage portion of Static Field does not effect enemy (Resistance debuff still refreshed)
