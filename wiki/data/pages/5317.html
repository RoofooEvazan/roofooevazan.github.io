This page provides various details about Low Level Dueling (LLD). For general details about PvP, see PvP Changes.

General Info

Low Level Dueling is a form of Player-vs-Player (PvP) combat that takes place in Normal Difficulty on characters at level(s) 9, 18, or 30.

The vast majority of LLD players are dueling with level 30 characters, though there are level 9 and 18 players that predominantly reside on Non-Ladder.

Unless otherwise stated, all information here can be assumed to reference LLD specifically as it pertains to level 30 characters.

All LLD characters are expected to be fully 'rushed', up to and including completing all relevant quests in Normal, Nightmare, and Hell.

Very frequently LLD is conducted in Team vs Team duels, especially in public games.

Crafted Items

To a large extent itemizing for any given build in LLD will, or can, include crafting.

In some cases a craft is directly competing with a Unique or Set item for an equipment slot.

Crafting is usually done with very specific resulting Item Levels, and thus Affix Levels, in mind. This is done by using characters with specific Character Levels and crafting bases with specific Item Levels. The intention being to limit the affix pool as much as possible to those with a level requirement of 30 or less.

Teleport Staves

For many builds, especially in certain duel match-ups, use of a staff weapon swap with Teleport charges is essentially necessary.

The easiest way to acquire a Teleport charges staff is through shopping.

These staves can be shopped as early as character level 19, shopping in Act 3+ in Normal or on a higher difficulty. The minimum Item Level necessary for the charges to potentially appear is 24.

That said, once you get to Nightmare, at certain Item Levels, a staff could potentially be sold as as an Exceptional, rather than a Normal, tier base. In this case it essentially comes with 20% more FCR than otherwise.

The Item Levels necessary for each respective Exceptional staff to potentially be shopped in Nightmare or Hell are as follows:

Jo Staff: 30 / Quarterstaff: 35 / Cedar Staff: 38 / Gothic Staff: 42 / Rune Staff: 47

There is logic, however, in shopping for these staves at a much higher character level. The higher the Item Level of the staff, the more charges available.

This heightened Item Level, and thus charge value, does not have a heightened character level requirement.

For the highest level of Teleport, and thus the most charges, one would need to shop at character level 92+ in Nightmare or Hell.

Crafting staves is also an option, though the probability of the Teleport charges affix appearing becomes incredibly low if you craft at higher Item Levels.

Some builds inevitably excel in Team-vs-Team (TvT) duels while others are better for One-vs-One (1v1) duels.
