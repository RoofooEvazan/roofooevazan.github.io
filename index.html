PD2 Calculators

Made by RoofooEvazan

Calculators and simulators for Project Diablo 2, built on the game's own code: the rules were reverse engineered from PD2's client files, and many were checked by running that code directly. Each tool shows how its numbers were found and what's still unverified.

Wiki

  • PD2 Wiki

    The whole Project Diablo 2 wiki, rebuilt for reading on any screen: instant search down to single runewords and skills, sortable and filterable tables, and a page outline that follows you. It re-syncs from the official wiki every day, and adds an interactive Act 2 merc weapon comparison.

    Instant search · Sortable tables · Synced daily · Mobile friendly · Merc weapon compare

    Open wiki Original wiki

Tools

  • IAS Calculator

    Frames per attack and breakpoints for every class, weapon, attack skill and speed skill, including werewolf and werebear speed.

    Breakpoints · Frame grid · Fastest frames · Wereforms

    Open calculator Source

  • Advanced Stats

    Every advanced stat of a character, loaded from a PD2 Armory export or a single-player save and read in your browser.

    Offense · Defense · Skill damage · Mercenary

    Open calculator Source

  • Hit Chance

    Your chance to hit a monster and a monster's chance to hit you, with block, dodge, avoid and evade, from PD2's own hit roll.

    Attack rating · Block · Dodge & evade · Attack rating needed

    Open calculator Source

  • Damage Atlas

    Every damage calculation, skill by skill, as flow charts: formulas, interactions and quirks traced from the code, with a pipeline map and a hit lab.

    Skill flow · Quirks · Pipeline map · Hit lab

    Open atlas Source

  • Spawn Simulator

    An experimental model of map spawns: monster density, packs, elites and estimated XP per map, plus a loot mini game that clears the map and shows where every drop fell.

    Density · Elites · XP · Loot run

    Open simulator Source

  • Base Finder

    The earliest area in the game where an item base can drop, and the earliest where it can roll the sockets you want, from PD2's own drop tables and item-level socket caps.

    Earliest drop · Max sockets · Area drop list · Item level

    Open finder Source

  • Loot Filter Builder

    Make Roofoo's loot filter your own without touching filter code: color themes, drop sounds, star tiers, mystery drops, and what shows or hides on each filter level.

    Themes · Drop sounds · Show / hide · Item tester

    Open builder Source

How to read these tools. Rules marked verified were checked by running the game's own code on many inputs; read means they come from reading the reverse-engineered code without running it. The spawn simulator is experimental and says where it's approximate.
